## Instrument

CTR transactional disc/executable provisioner

## Validated by

The shipping path accepted the real CTR USA CHD and reproduced the measured SYSTEM.CNF, 516096-byte SCUS_944.26, SHA-256, and header fields. The same path rejected a real unrelated Spider-Man 2 CHD before replacement. Its hermetic selftest also exercised CLI/game-env/generic-env/.env/sorted-drop-in precedence, invalid-explicit refusal, matching-executable acceptance, one-byte mutation rejection, and wrong SYSTEM.CNF rejection.

## Known failure modes

The resolver proves path precedence but does not identify media until extraction. The executable hash
identifies `SCUS_944.26`, not every other file on the disc. `discdump` must support the supplied image
and ISO9660 shape. This instrument establishes provisioning and executable identity only; it does not
prove a generated substrate, deterministic execution, or a booting port.
